# Follow Tea's section structure with Difratelli content

[Issue #34](https://github.com/danielluis07/difratelli-kids-v2/issues/34) supersedes the earlier homepage direction: follow Tea Collection's section order, carousels and editorial proportions, using Difratelli's Fraunces/DM Sans typography, palette and approved imagery/copy. The homepage, collection index/details and brand page run edge to edge; the 1440px cap continues on other routes. This records the owner's rejection of the earlier DESIGN.md/issue #28 hero mockup so later work does not restore its ivory box or four-card static homepage rows.

The hero is Difratelli's equal static split, with large white text and outlined actions directly over the lower-left photograph, one left photograph below 1024px and a local gradient for contrast. Omit Tea's “Inspired by the world” section and email signup; retain the existing sticky header and footer. Allow approved fictional non-price promotions and a pausable announcement rotation that stops under reduced motion; pricing changes, quick-add and external social links remain outside this decision. Content approval is bound to the immutable issue-34 review through `docs/content/homepage-approval.json`, with the owner's explicit conversational approval and subsequent implementation authorization recorded separately from storefront acceptance.
