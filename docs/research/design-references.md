# Kidswear storefront references

Research for [Research kidswear brands for design inspiration](https://github.com/danielluis07/difratelli-kids-v2/issues/2), under the [Difratelli Kids wayfinder map](https://github.com/danielluis07/difratelli-kids-v2/issues/1). Inspected 9 October 2026. This is a shortlist for a human design discussion, not a chosen direction or an implementation specification.

## Brief and evidence boundaries

Difratelli Kids is fictional: everyday clothing for girls and boys ages 2–8, comfort, playful colors and prints, accessible mid-range positioning. Initial categories: T-shirts, tops, shorts, trousers, dresses, matching sets, and light layers. Footwear is excluded initially; accessories remain undecided.

Observations come from official storefront text, navigation, image labels, and product metadata available through web extraction. Some sources are indexed snapshots rather than successful fresh fetches; campaigns, prices, and availability can change. Extracted document order does not prove rendered visual hierarchy.

**Visual limitations:** rendered screenshots were unavailable. Exact typefaces, logo treatment, UI palette, spacing, image crops, photography composition, hover behavior, and mobile layouts are unverified for every reference. Garment colors and prints below come from brand text and product names, not a visual audit. Proposed typography, palettes, and photography are interpretations for our brand, not observations of competitors. Product-detail inspection succeeded for Malwee; it was not completed for the other five brands.

## Three possible directions

These proposals synthesize the references. They are not decisions or claims about competitors' exact visual styles.

| Direction | Proposed visual and shopping character | Anchors | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Everyday color** | Clear sans-serif type, mostly light surfaces, a few bright accents, colorful garments, consistent product photos, simple category/size/color discovery. | Primary for color/mixing; Malwee for local shopping clarity. | Approachable and legible; needs original illustration or art direction to be distinctive. |
| **Brazilian play outdoors** | Warm light background, expressive headings, restrained organic shapes, original nature/fruit motifs, outdoor campaign photos, familiar retail information. | Fábula for imaginative naming; Tip Top for motifs; Malwee for purchase information. | Strong local personality; campaign photos must match fictional products. |
| **Little imaginary worlds** | One original collection story at a time, illustrated accents, expressive campaign imagery, a calm product grid with practical filters. | Bobo Choses for narrative; Hanna for coordinated prints/outfits. | Memorable showcase; may feel more boutique than intended without everyday products and accessible copy. |

## Remaining discussion

Choose which character feels right, then inspect linked sites visually with the human before locking typography, palette, photography, or homepage composition. Discuss gender/category navigation, promotion density, plain versus printed products, and coordinated outfits. These questions inform the future DESIGN.md decision ticket. This research does not author DESIGN.md or approve copying brand assets.
