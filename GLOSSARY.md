# Difratelli Kids

The fictional everyday kidswear offering presented by the Difratelli Kids storefront.

## Language

**Product**:
A distinct clothing style in the catalog, counted once regardless of its available colors and sizes.
_Avoid_: Using a color or size option as a separate product when describing catalog depth.

**Everyday separates**:
Individual T-shirts, tops, shorts, and trousers that shoppers can combine into outfits.

**Collection**:
A loose grouping of products sharing colors and prints; its individual products can be purchased separately.
_Avoid_: Using collection to mean a matching set.

**Matching set**:
A single product containing two coordinating clothing pieces sold together.
_Avoid_: Using matching set to mean an outfit assembled from separately sold products.

**Colorway**:
A color option of a product, including the combination of colors in a printed garment.

**Catalog imagery**:
Photographs representing a specific catalog product, including studio photographs of a child model wearing it and isolated garment photographs.

**Editorial imagery**:
Photographs expressing the brand and collection mood through everyday childhood scenes. Depicted clothing fits the store's assortment but need not represent a specific catalog product.

**Child model**:
A fictional child with a consistent visual identity who wears a batch of catalog products in the store's imagery.
_Avoid_: Using model to mean a clothing style in content discussions.

**Simulated checkout**:
A demonstration of completing a purchase that ends in a fictional confirmation, without a payment or real order.
_Avoid_: Describing the fictional confirmation as a real purchase or order.

**Favorite**:
A product saved by a visitor for later browsing, independently of its colorways and sizes.
_Avoid_: Treating a favorite as a selected purchasable option or a cart item.
