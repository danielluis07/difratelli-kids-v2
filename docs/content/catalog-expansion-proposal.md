### What is available and what is missing

- Color is already available beyond cream/teal: butter-yellow shorts, sky-blue tops/bottoms/dress, coral shorts, lilac tops/trousers/T-shirt and peach shorts/trousers/dress. Their limited prominence in editorial selections is a merchandising/photography issue, not an absence of color in the catalog.
- All five separately sold shorts and all four trousers are plain. More plain bottoms would add little initial value. Dresses, trousers and layers can already break the repeated T-shirt-and-shorts editorial silhouette.
- There is no plain T-shirt, no plain dress and no plain long-sleeve pullover. Plain upper-body choices are one cream boys' polo, two girls' sleeveless tops and one cardigan suitable for both. A standalone solid T-shirt is the strongest practical gap.
- Existing prints use repeated small motifs, chalk groups or stripes. Colored print bases exist, but no garment brief specifies one large, positioned narrative illustration. Larger illustration placement is a distinct visual gap.
- #51 added **two polos, of which only one is plain**. It replaced product-002 and product-011 rather than expanding the catalog. The cream polo is useful now; the striped polo must not be counted as another solid. The deferred red-bear editorial T-shirt is not an approved catalog product.
